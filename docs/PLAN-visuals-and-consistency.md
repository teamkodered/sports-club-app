## 2. Cleaner
- Less text on cards: long explanations behind a small ⓘ.
- Fewer duplicates: points, targets and weight each in one main place (links from the others).

- Wearable workouts become one-tap suggestions ("Boxing · 21 min · avg 82% -- Log it?"); nothing logged without a tap.

  PDP to-dos due, tests left, wearable suggestions to log.
- Weekly recap every Monday: last week's total, streak, league movement, best session.
- Streak badge on the profile; coach 👍 reactions on athlete logs / notes.
